# Data when using Agentnexos

This notice describes the public experience and local brief. Enterprise processing, providers, and retention are defined in the project agreement before a customer system is connected.

## Local brief

Project form answers stay in browser page memory and can be downloaded. The form does not submit a request to the team. Closing or clearing the page removes local state. Opening an email moves the text you choose to share into your email app; nothing is sent until you send it.

## Public conversations

Submitted assistant messages pass through the application on Vercel to the model provider Groq. Limited conversation context is included according to the application. Use fictional information and avoid secrets, customer records, or content you are not entitled to use.

## Operational records and usage limits

The application uses Supabase for reservations, run status, stage events, and usage limits. An HMAC-derived connection identifier supports quotas; a fingerprint does not encrypt the connection address or imply no infrastructure data exists.

Old records are cleaned through the current reservation mechanism. This notice does not guarantee a maximum retention period or scheduled deletion. Retention details must be reconciled with implementation and provider settings for each release.

## Measurement and providers

The project includes Vercel Analytics. Hosting, model, and measurement providers may process technical data under their services and configuration. The site makes no general promise of zero retention, no training, or processing in a particular country.

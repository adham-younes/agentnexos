# Using the public experience

Agentnexos provides a process design assistant and a local project brief. Using them does not establish a development contract, purchase order, or service booking.

## Examples and outputs

Examples are fictional, and outputs are proposals requiring review by the process owner and relevant specialists. A plan or illustrative approval status is not authorization to act in enterprise systems.

## Execution boundaries

The public experience does not pay, purchase, send email, or modify CRM or ERP. Connected-system development, sources, permissions, tests, delivery, and support are defined in the project agreement.

## Responsibility for data

Use fictional information and do not provide secrets or data you have no right to process. Do not attempt to access another party's data or bypass usage and permission limits.

## Sharing the brief

Downloading saves a copy for you. Opening an email to the project does not send it automatically; review before sending. Conversation use follows the [data notice](/en/privacy).

These are public-experience terms and require legal review and completion of entity details before commercial enterprise launch.

# System design assistant

Describe the process, systems you use, and intended output. The assistant helps organize requirements, propose a workflow, and identify data, permissions, and questions that change the design.

## Start with recurring work

- Design a procurement review process.
- Prepare follow-up for invoices in different states.
- Define sources for an internal knowledge assistant.
- Review a plan connecting a process to your systems.

## What it can provide here

Workflow design, gap checks in the definition, baseline workload calculations from your inputs, and selected technical references when available. A proposal remains a plan for process-owner approval.

## Before sending

Use fictional examples. Submitted conversations are processed through the model provider; read the [data notice](/en/privacy). Do not include secrets or customer records.

This is a public design experience. Enterprise tools, memory, and execution approvals are defined and implemented in a separate customer workspace within the project.

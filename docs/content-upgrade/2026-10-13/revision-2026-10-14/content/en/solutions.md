# Solutions that connect enterprise knowledge to completed work

Agents are useful when a task depends on dispersed information and needs interpretation or coordination before reaching a decision or action. The solution is defined around that task, then connected to its sources, rules, and systems of record.

## Enterprise knowledge

Retrieve permitted information, understand relationships, and prepare a sourced answer or analysis. The design covers versions, conflicts, and missing evidence. [Solution details](/en/solutions/knowledge)

## Procurement and supplier management

Assemble requirements, offer terms, and supplier information into a consistent comparison, separating recommendation, approval, and order creation. [Solution details](/en/solutions/procurement)

## Receivables operations

Organize invoices by payment, dispute, and communication status, and prepare appropriate follow-up for approval before external contact. [Solution details](/en/solutions/receivables)

## Customer operations

Assemble case context and identify an authorized resolution, passing exceptions to the responsible person with what they need to continue. [Solution details](/en/solutions/customer-operations)

## Choose the first solution

Start with recurring work, reliable sources, a decision owner, and an output that can be evaluated. The initial scope may cover reading and output preparation before expanding into bounded execution after access, policy, and recovery are proven.

These solutions are custom development scopes, not integrations enabled in the public experience. [Define your project](/en/start)

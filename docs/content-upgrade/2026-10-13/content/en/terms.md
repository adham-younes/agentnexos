# Website and public experience usage terms

The website introduces Agentnexos services and offers workflow design with fictional data. Enterprise scope, deliverables and support require a separate agreement.

## Outputs require responsible review

An assistant suggestion or brief does not grant approval or prove an external action completed. Review sources, calculations and assumptions before relying on an output. Do not use the public experience for purchase orders or financial or legal decisions.

## Use information you are authorized to handle

The public experience is intended for fictional data. Do not send personal or customer records, secrets or credentials, access other parties’ data, or bypass tool and usage limits.

## Experience and integration boundaries

The public experience has no email, CRM, ERP, purchase or payment tools. Connection availability and usage limits may affect responses. Integration availability, durable records and prevention of repeated external actions require implementation and testing.

## What a project agreement covers

The agreement defines scope, sources, permissions, approvals, acceptance, hosting, deliverables and support responsibility. Website examples do not replace these terms or represent customer results.

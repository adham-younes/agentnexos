# Agentnexos: engineering the connection between business needs and agent behavior

A project led by Adham Younes to develop AI agent systems and business process automation, with Arabic and English interfaces and human review for sensitive decisions.

## Founder and engineering focus

Adham Younes works on web applications, integrations and AI agent systems. Agentnexos focuses on translating business-team requirements into testable workflows with defined knowledge sources, permissions and review outputs.

The method starts with the task and selects an architecture to fit it. Multiple agents, knowledge graphs and persistent memory are engineering choices assessed for value and cost, rather than requirements added to every project.

## Arabic belongs in design and evaluation

Team terminology, document formats and request phrasing vary between Arabic and English. Demonstrating language quality requires domain cases, including abbreviations and expressions employees actually use.

## How an engagement is defined

An engagement has an agreed objective, scope, deliverables and acceptance criteria, with a process owner and technical representative. Public demonstrations are separated from work requiring company data, authorization or a dedicated environment.

Explore task design with the public assistant, then prepare a brief covering the goal, sources and acceptance criteria. Both tools are available with fictional information before scoping a connected system.

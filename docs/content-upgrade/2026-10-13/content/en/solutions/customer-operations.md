# Customer service: from case details to a reviewable resolution

Design a workflow that gathers customer context, identifies gaps and proposes the next step under service policy. Exceptions, compensation and account changes remain with authorized owners.

## Understand the case before replying

“My order has not arrived” needs an order ID, status, latest reliable update and possibly earlier case history. Define the sources and prevent another customer’s records from entering the response when names or references resemble each other.

## Collect, interpret and propose with separate execution

The proposed system reads authorized records, assembles a case summary and consults service policy to propose a response, clarification or escalation. The specialist can inspect the basis of the proposal.

If scope includes ticket creation or sending, each action receives identity, channel, validation and approval rules. A model suggestion alone does not resend a message or change order status.

## Escalation includes what the specialist needs

Disputes, compensation requests and conflicting status go to the responsible role with the issue, sources and clarifications already collected. Handoff should preserve context so the customer does not repeat the story.

Illustrative example: tracking says delivered and the customer reports non-receipt. The output flags the conflict and prepares an investigation ticket. It does not confirm successful delivery or promise unapproved compensation.

## Test the complete case

The pilot needs approved service policies, case data, customer identification and team permissions. Acceptance covers source accuracy, record protection, response suitability and escalation completeness. Arabic and English are evaluated against actual customer language.

The public interface supports design with fictional data. Support channels, CRM and customer records require a separate integration scope before handling real cases.

## Illustrative output

A fictional example of an output, not a customer record or executed action.

- Request: Customer reports non-receipt; record says delivered.
- Case file: Order reference, latest updates and conflicting status.
- Proposed action: Investigation ticket reviewed by the owner.
- Boundary: No delivery confirmation or compensation before resolution.

# Receivables follow-up grounded in the financial record

Organize follow-up, separate disputed invoices from overdue ones, and prepare messages the reviewer can reconcile. Finance defines prioritization, sending and escalation rules.

## Start with invoice status

An invoice list is not enough. Payment may be unposted, a credit note may apply, or a dispute may remain open. Following up before understanding status creates rework and confusion.

We identify the authoritative financial record, refresh timing and contact source. Scope defines the type of follow-up and the rules that hold a case for review.

## From due invoices to a follow-up draft

The proposed system collects invoice ID, due date, amount, currency, payment status and adjustments. Team rules prioritize cases and hold those with an uncertain balance or recipient.

A draft uses invoice details and previous follow-up context, with its supporting record shown to the reviewer. The team approves content and recipient before a configured channel sends it. Responses or escalation reasons are recorded when included in scope.

## Example: a partial payment needs reconciliation

An overdue balance appears, but the record contains a partial payment not linked to the invoice. The system highlights the conflict and asks the owner to confirm the balance. It does not request the full amount based only on the due date.

## Acceptance checks accuracy and exception behavior

- Match amount, currency and balance to the source; do not convert currencies without an explicit rule.
- Hold paid or disputed invoices until their status is resolved under policy.
- Respect contact permissions and record sending approval.
- Measure review time and drafts returned for correction.

## A defined financial scope

The solution supports information organization and follow-up preparation. Debt write-offs, credit decisions, settlements and fund transfers require separate authority. Approving a message does not authorize them. The public experience has no payment tool or connected financial ledger.

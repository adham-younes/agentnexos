# Your sector shapes the data and rules behind the solution

Our initial focus is commerce and distribution, professional services, and logistics. Within each sector, we choose measurable tasks linked to records and decision owners.

## Three distinct operating contexts

- Commerce and distribution: specifications, quotations, orders and receivables across customers, sales and inventory.
- Professional services: qualification, scope definition and handover from sales to delivery.
- Logistics: status tracking, exception handling and coordination between operations and customer service.

## A business function can serve several sectors

Procurement, receivables and internal knowledge recur across sectors. They sit under Solutions. Sector pages explain the differences in data sources, decision rules and operating context.

## What we assess before committing to scope

We assess system access, data quality, approval complexity and responsibility for errors. High-sensitivity tasks affecting safety or consequential personal decisions need specialist assessment and a different scope; they are outside these general operating examples.

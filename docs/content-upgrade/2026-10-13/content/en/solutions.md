# Solutions for tasks with an owner and an outcome

Identify where repeated messages, manual searches and reviews slow work down. Each solution explains its data, processing steps, human decisions and integration requirements.

## Procurement and request review

When requests arrive incomplete or quotations are hard to compare, begin with requirements and a consistent comparison. The proposed output is a request file and review notes before supplier commitments.

[Procurement review](/en/solutions/procurement)

## Receivables follow-up

Organize cases by due date, payment status and disputes, then prepare follow-up drafts that reconcile with the financial record. Sending depends on team approval and verified contact data.

[Receivables follow-up](/en/solutions/receivables)

## Enterprise knowledge

Employees need answers they can trace to sources they may access. Knowledge assistant design starts with policies, versions and sharing rules, followed by answer and escalation tests.

[Enterprise knowledge](/en/solutions/knowledge)

## Customer service and case handling

The proposed system collects authorized case context and presents the issue and suggested resolution. Compensation, exceptions and account changes remain separate, authorized actions.

[Customer operations](/en/solutions/customer-operations)

## Start with work you can test

A suitable task recurs, has a reference source, and has an owner who can recognize a correct result. Unresolved policies or unreliable records need to be addressed in the starting scope.

These are solutions to design and implement within a project. Examples illustrate workflows; they are not customer case studies or connected public-demo functions.

# Professional services: preserve the brief from enquiry to delivery

Scattered conversations and files delay scope definition and repeat clarification. The proposed system prepares a handover file the delivery team can use.

## Qualify the request before making commitments

Intake collects the goal, expected deliverables, constraints, timing and decision owners. Gaps and conflicts are reviewed before the enquiry becomes a scoped opportunity. The agent does not commit pricing or delivery dates for the team.

## A handover preserves decision context

The output contains requirements, references, approved decisions, open questions and exclusions. Changed requests show what changed and what needs review instead of silently altering the earlier agreement.

Start with a fictional internal brief, then extend to file sources and tracking after defining sharing boundaries. Uploading or sending a file is a separate action from summarizing it.

## Use team knowledge with controlled sharing

Service guides, templates and permitted lessons can help prepare proposals. Customer files remain separate from general material, with access checked before referencing another engagement.

## Measure handover completeness

Compare the file with agreed requirements and test that constraints remain intact and suggestions do not become commitments. Time and clarification rounds use comparable cases before and after the pilot.

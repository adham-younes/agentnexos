# Commerce and distribution: connect enquiries to the information sales needs

Between an enquiry and a response sit specifications, prices, inventory and supply terms. Start by organizing those inputs for sales, purchasing and receivables decisions.

## From an incomplete enquiry to a quotation request

A customer may use a name absent from the catalog or request a quantity without size. The proposed workflow collects use requirements, specifications and quantity, matches approved product information and requests pricing-relevant details.

The output is a quotation request or draft with source dates. Special prices, discounts and substitutes need rules and permissions. Stock or delivery dates must be confirmed by the authoritative system.

## Practical starting scopes

- Collect quotation requirements for the sales owner.
- Review supply requests and quotations for a defined category.
- Prepare receivables follow-up while separating returns and disputes.
- Answer team questions from current catalogs and supply policies.

## Start with one product category and an approved price rule

Identify the catalog, price list, inventory source and quote approver. Test unit, currency and specification differences and price updates; measure how many requests reach review without further information gathering.

This is a custom solution scope, not a ready-made inventory product or validated demand forecast. Expansion follows record quality and pilot results.

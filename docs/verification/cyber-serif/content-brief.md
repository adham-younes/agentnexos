# Audience and content direction

For operations managers, founders and process owners in Arabic-first businesses across Egypt and the Gulf. They need to decide which recurring process is suitable, understand what they can try now and know what remains to validate before enterprise operation.
Promise: turn a vague automation idea into a reviewable workflow plan. Mechanism: describe the process in the bounded assistant, identify inputs, decisions, sources and approvals; prepare a local project brief; use a bounded pilot before enterprise connection.
Evidence: implemented bilingual workflow design demo, actual health check, local-only brief validation/download, existing limited tools and tests. No customer endorsements, performance numbers, certifications or live CRM integration claims.
Tone: professional human Arabic and equivalent English, concrete practical examples, short headings and useful explanations. Technical details belong in guides/security rather than overwhelming the landing page.
Narrative: process friction → clear design promise → illustrative purchasing workflow → capabilities tied to decisions → three explorable use cases → discover/design/validate method → honest availability matrix → privacy and control → questions → prepare brief or try assistant.
Primary next action: open /agentnexos and use fictional details. Secondary next action: /start to create a local Markdown brief without submission.
Visual: approved Cyber Serif: black, emerald detail, Newsreader English display, Inter body, Space Grotesk labels, Arabic companion face, restrained glass panels and one illustrative workflow scene. No fake progress, count-ups, operational status or benchmark numbers.

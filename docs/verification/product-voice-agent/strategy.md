# Product, content and experience brief

Audience: operations owners, finance/procurement leads and technical sponsors in Egypt and the Gulf. Their question is which recurring task can become a useful agent workflow, what data it needs, and who retains the decision.

Agentnexos identity: Arabic-first platform direction for custom enterprise agent systems. The available public product supports workflow discovery/design, deterministic checks/calculations and a browser-only project brief. Production enterprise execution remains a scoped implementation requiring verified identity, narrow permissions and saved approvals. Marketing must distinguish that direction from today's public preview.

Primary promise: design an agent system around a concrete operational workflow, with reviewable outputs and named decisions. Entry: describe a process to the assistant. Deeper preparation: create a local brief, review with the process owner, choose a bounded pilot. No invented sales contact endpoint, customers, certifications, pricing or savings.

Research, accessed13October2026:
- Relevance AI: https://relevanceai.com/ frames discovery, tailored agents and evaluated pilots. Apply a clear path from use case to pilot; do not transplant its ROI, deployment timelines or customers.
- Dust: https://dust.tt/ connects company context, tools and people around workflows and describes governance separately. Apply source/role/result clarity; do not claim its connectors or certifications.
- Lindy: https://www.lindy.ai/ starts from recognizable jobs and role-specific use cases. Apply task-level labels and concrete examples; no claims of live connected actions.
- n8n: https://n8n.io/ and https://docs.n8n.io/build/integrate-ai/ai-examples/human-in-the-loop-for-tools.md distinguish AI workflow design from explicit human approval for tool actions. Keep approval in the design and authority in deterministic code.

Interpretation: lead with the product and user task, illustrate a real-shaped request, explain the mechanism, disclose what is available, then offer the next useful action. This is a content/design inference from public messaging, not a user-research finding or conversion forecast.

Voice: natural professional Arabic, matched English intent. Precise verbs and useful details; no invented founding story, forced triads, repeated not-X-but-Y contrasts or decorative slogans. Legal facts and provider disclosures remain intact. Humanizer pass edits meaning and rhythm in context, not just adjectives.

Visual direction: keep approved Cyber Serif type/palette/effects. Preserve grid/container edges and balanced paired content. Improve readability by limiting heading width, adding field examples and placing action/result copy at the decision. Purposeful asymmetry is acceptable; literal mirror symmetry is not an acceptance goal. Capture before/after and test localized wrapping.
